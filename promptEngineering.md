## Prompt Engineering

### Important to understand 
- Simple tasks VS Complex Tasks.
- Complex Tasks requires more complex prompts, you need the AI to follow your rules, patterns and quality standards.

### How System Prompt works?
- Specify the rules: "Work with python 1.3"
- Set the tone of the conversation and answers.
- Some tools don't open system prompt to change.
- [O que é system prompt](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/o-que-e-system-prompt-entenda-as-regras-nas-respostas-das-ias/)

### Define a Role for the IA
- This is not mandatory but this could help to get better answers. `as a developer` the IA must focus more on technical aspects of the answer. `as a product owner` the answer would have a tone with more business answer.
- If you want to write simple tasks this step could be ignored. But if you need more complex tasks to be solved, defining a rule would be very important to define the answer.

### Be specific
- Be specific, simple, clear.
- Review your prompt and remove unnecessary words.
- Read/send your prompt to another person could review. If other people don't understand then the AI will not understand as well.
- don't use words you don't use in daily conversations.

### Don't leave room for interpretations or assumptions 
- Think that: the AI needs the same answer you do.
